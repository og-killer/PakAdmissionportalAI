🎓 UniPath AI — Smart University Admission Predictor

Your Future. Your University. Your Path.

UniPath AI is a smart, student-friendly web application designed to help Pakistani students explore university admission opportunities based on their academic performance. It combines admission prediction, university exploration, and entry-test preparation in one platform.

Our goal is to make the university admission process easier, more accessible, and less confusing for students.

🚀 Project Overview

Choosing the right university and degree program can be challenging for students in Pakistan. Students often struggle to understand admission eligibility, calculate merit aggregates, compare universities, and prepare for entrance examinations.

UniPath AI aims to solve these problems by providing a centralized platform where students can explore universities, estimate their admission chances, and prepare for entry tests.

✨ Key Features

📊 1. Admission Predictor

* Enter Matric, Intermediate, and entry-test marks.
* Calculate an estimated admission aggregate.
* Explore degree programs based on eligibility.
* Compare student aggregates with previous closing merit.
* View estimated admission chances.

🏫 2. University Explorer

* Explore Pakistani universities.
* Search universities by name, location, and field.
* Discover degree programs such as:
    * BS Computer Science
    * BS Software Engineering
    * BS Artificial Intelligence
    * BS Information Technology
    * Engineering and other available programs.
* View admission requirements and merit information.

📝 3. University-Specific Test Preparation

* Explore university entry-test patterns.
* Access available official sample papers and practice resources.
* Practice subject-wise multiple-choice questions.
* Prepare for Mathematics, English, Analytical Reasoning, and other relevant subjects.

⏱️ 4. Interactive Mock Tests

* Attempt practice tests.
* Navigate between questions.
* Submit answers and calculate scores.
* Review performance by subject.
* Identify areas that need improvement.

🤖 5. AI Admission Assistant

The AI assistant is designed to help students with:

* Understanding admission requirements.
* Exploring suitable universities and programs.
* Understanding aggregate calculations.
* Preparing for university entrance tests.
* Generating practice questions.
* Receiving personalized study recommendations.

AI-generated guidance is supplementary and does not replace official university admission information.

🎯 6. Personalized Recommendations

Help students identify potentially suitable programs based on their academic profile, eligibility, available merit data, and test-preparation performance.

🗄️ Database Overview

The planned database structure includes:

Collection	Purpose
Universities	University names, locations, and information
Programs	Degree programs and eligibility requirements
Merit Formulas	University-specific aggregate calculation weights
Entry Tests	Test patterns, durations, and requirements
Questions	Practice MCQs and answer information
Study Resources	Official resources and practice materials
Test Results	Student mock-test performance

The database is designed to support structured university information and make it easier to add more universities and programs in the future.

🔄 How It Works

1. The student enters their academic marks.
2. The application checks the selected program’s eligibility requirements.
3. The system retrieves the relevant university merit formula.
4. The application calculates the estimated aggregate.
5. The student explores potentially suitable universities and programs.
6. The student accesses relevant entry-test preparation resources.
7. The student attempts mock tests and reviews their performance.
8. The AI assistant provides additional guidance where available.

⚠️ Disclaimer

UniPath AI provides admission estimates and educational guidance. Admission chances are not guaranteed and depend on official eligibility requirements, university-specific merit formulas, available seats, admission policies, and competition.

University information and previous closing merit must be verified against official sources. Sample data must be clearly labeled as demo data, and practice questions must not be misrepresented as official examination papers.

Students should always consult the relevant university’s official website for the latest admission information.
